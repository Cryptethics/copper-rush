What is Copper Rush?

Copper Rush is a fast-paced arcade mining game built around short, repeatable runs. Explore the mine, collect valuable resources, build combos and survive until the timer ends.

Each run uses a different map progression and a daily gameplay modifier, so adapting your route and decisions is part of the challenge.

Copper

🪙

Main Resource

Main scoring resource of every run.

Copper is the main resource found throughout the mine.

Collecting copper increases your run score. Fast consecutive pickups also build your combo multiplier, making each additional pickup more valuable.

Diamonds are rare pickups that provide much more value than standard copper. Each diamond has a color that decides how it changes your score.

  • Blue — adds a flat bonus to your score. Comes in +50, +75 and +100 variants. Most common, no per-run limit.
  • Green — multiplies your total run score. Comes in 1.25x and 1.5x variants. Rare, capped per run.
  • Gold — 2x multiplier. Rare, capped per run. Stacks with Green.
  • Black — triggers a full JACKPOT event. Extremely rare, capped per run.

Green and Gold multipliers stack, so collecting both in the same run can dramatically boost your final score.

☢️

Gas

Toxic gas is the main threat in Copper Rush. It spreads through the mine and gradually reduces the safe playable area.

The gas becomes more dangerous as the run continues. If it reaches the player, the run ends immediately.

⚡

Power-ups

🧲

Magnet

Pulls nearby copper toward the player for a limited time.

⚡

Speed Boost

Temporarily increases movement speed.

Two power-ups are available during a run:

  • Magnet — pulls nearby copper toward the player for a limited time.
  • Speed Boost — temporarily increases movement speed.

Each power-up becomes available during the run and can only be used once, so timing matters.

🔥

Combo & Frenzy

Combo x3
FRENZY

Collect copper quickly without long pauses to build a combo multiplier. Higher combos increase the value of collected copper.

During the final seconds, Frenzy begins: copper value increases, but the gas becomes more aggressive.

🏆

Score

🪙Copper + 💎Diamonds + 🔥Combo + ✦Multipliers = 🏆 Final Score

The final run score is based on the resources collected and the active bonuses earned during the run.

Copper, diamonds, combo multipliers and Frenzy performance all contribute to the final result.

🎲

Daily Mutator

⛏

Dense Copper

Bigger, brighter copper. Worth 3 each.

About 40% more copper spawns, and roughly 15% of it becomes rich copper worth 3× a normal pickup.

💎

Diamond Day

Diamonds spawn faster. Rare caps unchanged.

Seven diamond spawns during the run instead of the usual five; the limit on active diamonds is unchanged.

💨

Fast Gas

Gas accelerates faster. Frenzy unchanged.

The gas ramps up sooner, shrinking the safe area faster than normal. Frenzy timing is not affected.

🔥

High Risk

Risk zones pay big. Gas hunts harder there.

Copper inside risk zones is worth 4× instead of the usual 3×, but the gas closes on those areas more aggressively.

Each UTC day, all players receive the same Daily Mutator. It changes one important rule of the mine and requires players to adapt their strategy.
